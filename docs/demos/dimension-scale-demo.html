Dimension scale demo

The idea

Draw without caring about sizes. The first dimension does not move anything: it only says what the line you drew is worth, and that sets the scale. Every later dimension is relative to it.

Try this

  1. The example rectangle is drawn about 9 × 5 grid squares. Pick Dimension, click the bottom edge and type 200. Nothing moves: the 200 has set the scale.
  2. Dimension the left side 150. Only the height changes, to 150/200 of the bottom.
  3. Click the 200 and type 400. With another dimension there, it now drives its line like any other: the bottom becomes twice as long and the height stays 150.
  4. Delete both dimensions: click a number and press Delete beside its box, or Delete dimension at the top, or right-click the number. With none left, the next one sets a new scale again.
  5. While the first dimension is the only one, changing it moves nothing: it only renames the scale.

Drag points with Move: dimensioned lines keep their length, the rest follow. The filled point is where each connected part is held.

Export

Export preview (P) shows the figure as it is exported. The numbers play no part in its size: whatever the drawing looks like is fitted, dimensions and all, to the figure width, with 5 % of the width as a margin on every side. Text stays 10 pt and arrowheads keep their size; only the drawing is scaled. Change the width and watch it refit. Download SVG saves exactly that.

This is a stand-in for the real solver: lines keep the direction they were drawn in where they can, and only lengths are dimensioned.
